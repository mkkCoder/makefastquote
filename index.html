Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is nowhere to sign up, because there is no server to sign up to. Open the editor and start typing.

Where are my documents stored?

In your browser, on the device you are using. That means they survive a refresh and are waiting for you tomorrow — but they do not follow you to another computer, and clearing your browser data clears them. Export to CSV or JSON if you want a copy you keep.

What does Pro actually change?

Your logo goes in the header, three more templates unlock, and the small credit line in the footer comes off. Everything else — unlimited documents, PDF export, tax, discounts, signatures — is in the free version and stays there.

Is $29 really one payment?

Yes. One payment, no renewal, no card kept on file. You get a code by email; paste it in once and this browser is unlocked.

Can I use it for client work in any currency?

Ten currencies are built in, and per-line tax rates cover VAT, GST and sales tax. The PDF font covers Latin-script languages; if you need Hebrew, Arabic or CJK, it is not there yet.
