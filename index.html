Questions

Quote and estimate generator FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. There is nowhere to sign up, because there is no server to sign up to.

Where are my documents stored?

In your browser, on this device. They survive a refresh and are waiting tomorrow — but they don't follow you to another computer, and clearing browser data clears them. Export to CSV or JSON if you want a copy you keep.

What does Pro actually change?

Your logo, brand colour, three more templates, and the footer credit comes off. Everything else is in the free version and stays there.

Which currencies and languages?

Ten currencies, and per-line tax rates covering VAT, GST and sales tax. Latin-script languages and Hebrew render as real, selectable PDF text. Arabic and CJK are not supported yet.

Can I generate a quote, estimate, proposal, or proforma?

Yes. Switch the document type in the editor. These are commercial quotations — not tax invoices. Official tax invoices should be issued separately after payment or project completion.

Is the PDF searchable text or just an image?

Real, selectable text. Your client can copy bank details from the file. The preview and the PDF are drawn from one model, so they match.

Is MakeFastQuote a free quote generator?

Yes. Unlimited quote and estimate PDFs are free. Pro is a one-time $29 unlock for logo, brand colour, studio templates, and removing the footer credit.
