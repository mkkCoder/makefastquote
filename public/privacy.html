Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Who we are

MakeFastQuote is a browser-based quote and proposal generator at makefastquote.com. Billing contact is via Lemon Squeezy only — see Contact. We do not publish an operator email.

What we collect

  • In the app: Content you type (company details, line items, notes, logos, signatures) stays in your browser storage on this device (localStorage). We do not operate an app server that receives your documents.
  • When you buy Pro: Payment and licence fulfilment are handled by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record). Their privacy policy applies to checkout. Licence keys may be validated in the browser via Lemon Squeezy’s License API.
  • Analytics: None. We do not use analytics cookies or trackers.

What we do not do

We do not require an account, sell your document content, or run a database of your quotes or proposals. Exports (PDF, CSV, JSON) are created on your device.

Data retention & deletion

Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes local content and may remove a locally stored Pro unlock. Keep your licence key from the purchase email so you can restore Pro on this or another device.

Contact

See Contact for Lemon Squeezy billing and refunds. There is no operator email or product-support inbox.
